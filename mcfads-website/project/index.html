02

How It Works

From first call to switched-on system

The same four-step process whether you're down the road or in a different country — we design remotely and coordinate installation locally.

01

Consultation & load assessment

We talk through what you actually need to keep running — appliances, business equipment, hours of backup — and estimate your real power load.

02

Site survey & system design

An engineer assesses your roof, wiring, and available space, then designs a system sized correctly — not oversold or undersized.

03

Installation & commissioning

Panels, inverter, and battery bank installed and wired by our team, tested under load before we call it done.

04

Handover & ongoing support

A full walkthrough of your system, plus monitoring and maintenance support long after installation day.
